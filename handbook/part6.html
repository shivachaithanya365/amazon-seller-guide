Appendix B · Closing Fee Groups

Below ₹500 your closing fee depends on which list your fee category sits in — and the two price bands have separate lists. From ₹501 upward the lists stop mattering: every category pays the same.

It is tempting to read this appendix as three tiers, each with a cheap rate and a dearer one. That is wrong. The ₹301–500 lists are not a relabelling of the ₹0–300 lists.

Amazon’s own worked example on the fee page makes the point: Apparel - Shorts is in Group A (₹20) below ₹300, but at ₹450 it is billed under Group D at ₹14 — not the ₹18 a Group A → Group C pairing would predict.
